Since 2008 Eclipse, Inc. has specialized in Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) HVAC systems, offering comprehensive solutions for commercial and industrial applications. 

Our installation of a VRF system in the First National Plaza building in Cloquet
was part of a research project by Minnesota Power, and was one of the first
installations of such a system in the state. You can 
[read Minnesota Power’s Research Profile on this VRF system here.](https://minnesotapower.blob.core.windows.net/content/Content/Documents/EnergyConservation/Business/CustomerProfiles/vrfz.pdf)

As a leading contractor in the Duluth region, we provide complete VRF services including design, installation, maintenance, and system optimization.

VRF systems represent the pinnacle of modern HVAC technology, offering:
- Superior energy efficiency through variable-speed operation
- Precise zone-by-zone temperature control
- Simultaneous heating and cooling capabilities
- Reduced operational costs
- Flexible installation options
- Advanced climate control management

Our experienced teams work closely with architects, building owners, and facility managers to design and implement VRF solutions that maximize comfort while minimizing energy consumption. From initial assessment through ongoing maintenance, we ensure your VRF system delivers optimal performance year-round.
