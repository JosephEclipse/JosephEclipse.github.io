# Style 1: Professional & Technical

Eclipse Inc. delivers comprehensive millwork solutions from our 50,000-square-foot production facility, serving commercial and hospitality clients throughout the greater Duluth region. Our expert craftsmen combine traditional woodworking skills with modern manufacturing processes to create exceptional architectural elements and custom installations.

Our millwork capabilities include:
- Custom commercial cabinetry
- Solid surface and laminate countertops
- Interior and exterior trim packages
- Specialty moldings and custom profiles
- Decorative columns and posts
- Exterior wood siding solutions including log and shiplap
- Interior architectural specialties

Each project benefits from our integrated approach to design, fabrication, and installation, ensuring seamless execution from concept to completion. Our commitment to quality craftsmanship, paired with our extensive production capabilities, enables us to handle projects of any scale while maintaining precise attention to detail.
