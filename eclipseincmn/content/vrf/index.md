Instead of traditional HVAC systems, Eclipse focuses on VRF systems for their
energy-saving potential. We have worked on these systems since 2008, when we
installed a VRF system in the First National Plaza building in Cloquet. This
job was part of a research project by Minnesota Power, and was one of the first
installations of such a system in the state. You can 
[read Minnesota Power’s Research Profile on this VRF system here.](https://minnesotapower.blob.core.windows.net/content/Content/Documents/EnergyConservation/Business/CustomerProfiles/vrfz.pdf)
